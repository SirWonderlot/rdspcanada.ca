Program 3 of 5

Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP)

A long-term savings plan. The government can add money through two programs:

  • Canada Disability Savings Grant: matches contributions up to 300%, depending on income. Up to $3,500 a year ($10,500 with unused past years), $70,000 lifetime.
  • Canada Disability Savings Bond: up to $1,000 a year with no contributions needed, $20,000 lifetime.

Requirements

Program 4 of 5

Canada Pension Plan Disability (CPP-D)

A monthly payment for people under 65 who have paid into the CPP and can't work regularly because of a severe and prolonged disability. In 2026: a basic $610.46 a month, up to $1,741.20 depending on contributions.

Requirements

Age
18 to 64
CPP contributions
In 4 of the last 6 years, or 3 of the last 6 with at least 25 years of contributions, or under the late applicant provision
Disability
Severe and prolonged; regularly prevents working
Disability Tax Credit
Not required

Applicant's qualifiers

Late applicant provision: a person can apply years later if they had enough CPP contributions when they became disabled, and have been continuously disabled since. For example, someone disabled at 45 who applies at 55 must show they were disabled at 45 and have remained disabled the whole time.

Program 5 of 5

Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

A one-time, tax-free payment (minimum $10,000) to a person's loved ones at the time of their passing. Participants make a monthly contribution, typically $1 to $2 a day, guaranteed never to increase.
