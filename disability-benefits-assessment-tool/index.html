Disability Benefits Assessment Tool

Before you begin, please answer one question below.

Start here

Have you qualified for the Disability Tax Credit (DTC)?

The DTC is required for the RDSP and most of the benefits programs on the site, so it is the first thing to check. Please indicate whether you have qualified for the Disability Tax Credit certificate.

You can proceed

DTC approval is a requirement for the following programs. You may be eligible for:

  • Canada Disability Savings GrantUp to $70,000 in matching contributions.
  • Canada Disability Savings BondUp to $20,000, with no deposits needed.
  • Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP)The account that holds the Grant and Bond.
  • Disability AmountA $10,341 tax credit (2026), transferable to family.
  • Child Disability BenefitUp to $290 a month for each eligible child.
  • Canada Disability BenefitUp to $204.20 a month, ages 18 to 64.
  • Home buying and accessibilityThe Home Buyers' Amount and Home Accessibility Tax Credit.

The Disability Tax Credit comes first

DTC approval is required before you can open an RDSP or collect the Disability Savings Bond and Grant, and for most of the other benefits programs. It is for people whose health condition has lasted, or is expected to last, at least 12 months.

What DTC approval can mean

You don't need an income to benefit. Benefits marked No income needed are paid even if you have little or no income.

Apply for the Disability Tax Credit

The DTC Application Assistance Program helps you prepare a complete application from plain-language questions, including guidance for your medical practitioner, to give you the best opportunity for approval.

Apply for the DTC at dtcc.ca
