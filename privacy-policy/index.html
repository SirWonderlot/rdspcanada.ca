Privacy policy

RDSP Canada is a free information service. We don't ask for, collect, or sell personal information.

In short:

  • You can use this website without an account and without giving us any personal information.
  • We don't use cookies. We count visits with privacy-friendly analytics that never identify you.
  • We don't run advertising, and we don't sell, rent, or share information with anyone.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you.

This policy explains how RDSP Canada (rdspcanada.ca) handles information. It applies to this website.

1. Information we don't collect

We never ask for your name, address, Social Insurance Number (SIN), date of birth, health information, or banking details. When you are eligible for a program, we link you directly to the official government application so that you give your information only to the program itself.

2. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This website does not set cookies. To understand how the site is used and improve it, we use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. It counts things like which pages are visited, which page a visit ends on, the type of device, the country, and how often our main links are clicked. It does not store your IP address, track you across other websites, or record the answers you give in the assessment tool.

There is no advertising on this site, and we do not accept payment from anyone to promote a program or service.

3. Website hosting

Like every website, this site is delivered by a hosting provider. When you visit a page, the hosting provider's servers may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address and the time of the request, for security and to keep the service running. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors.

4. Email

If you email us at benefits@rdspcanada.ca, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you, and we do not add you to any mailing list. Please do not send sensitive information such as your SIN or banking details by email.

To have your correspondence deleted, email us and ask.

5. The Guaranteed Access Program health questionnaire

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) health questionnaire at access.rdspcanada.ca is separate from this information website. Unlike this website, it does collect personal information, including health information, contact details, and date of birth, but only if you choose to complete it and give your consent on the questionnaire.

That information is used to determine the GAP benefit amount you qualify for and to prepare for your review with a Disability Benefits Specialist. The questionnaire explains, before you submit it, who receives your information and how it is used.

6. Booking calendar

The booking calendar for your questionnaire review is operated by another company (Calendly). It loads from the provider's own servers. Anything you enter in it is handled under Calendly's privacy policy, not ours.

7. Links to other websites

We link to official federal, provincial, and territorial government websites and to other websites, including the DTC Application Assistance Program at dtcc.ca. When you follow a link, you leave this website, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

8. Children

Parents, guardians, and caregivers often use this website on behalf of a child. Because we do not collect personal information, no information about children is collected through this website.

9. Security

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date below.

11. An independent information service

RDSP Canada is an independent information service and is not affiliated with the Government of Canada. Program rules and amounts can change, so always confirm details with the official program source.

12. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about privacy on this website, contact us:
