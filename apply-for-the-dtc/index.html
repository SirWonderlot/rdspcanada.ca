Recommendations

There are three recommendations below. The recommendation highlighted in green is likely the optimal path for the applicant.

To review the benefits of qualifying for the DTC, visit here.

  • Apply for the disability tax credit independently

    The applicant is responsible for completing the entire form on their own.

    Pros

    • The applicant keeps 100% of any refund received from the CRA and all of their benefits.

    Cons

    • No professional guidance.
    • Has the highest rate of denials.

  • Apply for the disability tax credit using professional assistance (Recommended)

    A professional prepares the application from the applicant's answers, in the terms the CRA assesses, for the applicant and their medical professional to review and sign.

    Pros

    • One flat fee of $49 (requires referral code; regular price is $149), returned in full if the applicant does not qualify.
    • The applicant keeps 100% of any refund received from the CRA (up to $40,000) and all of their benefits.
    • Done entirely online, from the comfort of home, on any phone or computer.
    • The completed 16-page application is crafted to match the keywords and terms the CRA uses for approval.

    Referral code: RDSP49 (applied automatically)

  • Apply for the disability tax credit through a DTC company

    A company prepares and submits the application on the applicant's behalf, and charges a percentage of the benefits received.

    Pros

    • The company handles the application and follow-up with the CRA.

    Cons

    • Typically 25% or more of the refund and benefits, plus tax. On a $5,000 refund, that is $1,250 or more, plus tax.
    • The applicant usually authorizes the company to deal with the CRA on their behalf.
