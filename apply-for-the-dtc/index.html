Apply for the DTC

Because qualifying for the Disability Tax Credit is so important for persons living with long-term medical conditions, RDSP Canada has Benefits Specialists who will assist you with applying.

What qualifying can mean

After qualifying for the Disability Tax Credit, you may be eligible to receive each of the following.

  • Canada Disability BenefitUp to $204.20 a month
  • Canada Disability Savings GrantUp to $70,000 deposited into your RDSP
  • Canada Disability Savings BondUp to $20,000 deposited into your RDSP
  • Refund from the CRAUp to $40,000, for up to 10 past tax years
  • Disability amount$10,341 a year on your tax return (2026)

Schedule your benefits review session

  • Your Benefits Specialist will go through each benefits program with you to make sure you are receiving all of your government entitlements.
  • They will walk you through the DTC application process, show you the options you have when applying, and answer any of your questions.
  • If needed, your Benefits Specialist will assist you in applying for the Disability Tax Credit.
  • There are no fees to speak with a Benefits Specialist.
