True North Disability Services

True North Disability Services (TNDS) is a disability advocate and specialized tax consultant service that works with Disability Tax Credit Certificate candidates, their medical professionals and the Canada Revenue Agency, to assist with qualifying the candidate for the Disability Tax Credit Certificate and maximize their potential financial benefits gained through qualification.
