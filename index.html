The Disability Tax Credit comes first

DTC approval is required before you can open an RDSP or collect the Disability Savings Bond and Grant, and for most of the other benefits programs. It is for people whose health condition has lasted, or is expected to last, at least 12 months.

Disability benefits programs: information and applications

How do I collect my benefits?

The process

Collecting disability benefits takes four steps.

  1. Eligibility

    Find out whether you are eligible for each program. Every program has its own qualification criteria. You can check with the Disability Benefits Assessment Tool.

  2. Confirmation

    Receive confirmation of your eligibility. If you are not eligible, you will be told why and pointed to resources that may help, where available. Direct links to each program are provided.

  3. Apply

    If you are eligible, you must apply for each benefit. Some programs allow unclaimed amounts from previous eligible years to be collected retroactively.

  4. Collect

    Once you have applied and been approved, you can begin receiving the benefit. Some programs require you to reapply at set intervals to keep receiving it.

Please note: RDSP Canada does not collect any personal information. If you are eligible for a program, we give you a direct link to its official online application. This protects your privacy and keeps you secure while you explore these programs.

How much does this cost?

Fees

There are no fees to use this service. It is free for Canadians living with a prolonged medical condition.
