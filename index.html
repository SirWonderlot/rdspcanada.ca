What benefits programs are accessible?

Disability benefits programs

Several federal and provincial disability benefits programs are available to people living with long-term medical conditions. They include the following:

  • Canada Disability Benefit (CDB)Monthly income payments
  • Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)Free dental care
  • Canada Disability Savings Bond (CDSB)Up to $20,000
  • Canada Disability Savings Grant (CDSG)Up to $70,000
  • Canada Pension Plan Disability (CPP-D)Monthly income payments
  • Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)One-time lump-sum payout
  • Disability AmountTransferable credit for annual refunds
  • Provincial benefits programsPrograms in each province and territory
