Ontario disability support programs

Provincial supports for people living with disabilities in Ontario.

Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)

ODSP provides health and disability-related benefits that can help with many kinds of costs, including health, dental, vision, assistive devices, and other expenses related to a disability or medical condition.

For a single person, ODSP Income Support provides up to $1,436 a month for basic needs and shelter (since July 2026).

ODSP: information and application

Assistance for Children with Severe Disabilities (ACSD)

If you are a parent or guardian caring for a child with a severe disability, ACSD can help cover some of the extra costs of caring for your child.

ACSD: information and application

Assistive Devices Program (ADP)

Helps people with long-term physical disabilities pay for customized equipment, such as wheelchairs and hearing aids.

ADP: information and application

Developmental Services Ontario (DSO)

DSO is the access point for adult developmental services funded by the Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services (MCCSS). There are nine DSO locations across the province.

When a person turns 18, children's developmental services such as Special Services at Home, ACSD, and the Ontario Autism Program end. They must then apply through DSO to find out whether they are eligible for adult supports in their community.

DSO: information and application

Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) provides a one-time, tax-free payout to a person's loved ones when they pass away.

It is available to individuals who hold a valid Disability Tax Credit (DTC) certificate or who receive benefits through a federal or provincial disability support program. It is offered through a Canadian insurer.

Participants contribute a small amount each month in return for a one-time lump-sum payment (minimum $10,000) to the person of their choosing.

GAP: information and application

Ontario Works

If you are in financial need and require help with basic expenses such as food and housing, you may be eligible for Ontario Works. It provides:

  • Financial assistance with basic living costs, including food and rent, for you and your eligible family members
  • Health benefits for you and your dependants
  • Employment supports such as resume workshops, job counselling, job-specific training, and basic education programs

The amount you receive depends on your circumstances. In most cases, you must take part in employment-related activities to keep receiving support. For example, a single person could receive up to $733 a month for basic needs and shelter.

Ontario Works: information and application

Special Services at Home (SSAH)

Helps families caring for a child with a developmental and/or physical disability. It provides funding for services and supports that help parents care for their child, or help the child take part in activities at home and in the community.

SSAH: information and application

Ontario Autism Program (OAP)

Support for families of children and youth on the autism spectrum. Children and youth diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) by a qualified professional are eligible, and receive services and supports until age 18.

OAP: information and application
