Additional programs

Government programs beyond disability benefits.

This page is being updated. We are adding a broader range of government programs that are not specific to disability supports.

When complete, this section will provide clear explanations and direct links to federal and provincial programs that may offer financial assistance, education funding, tax benefits, housing supports, family programs, employment services, and other public resources.

Our goal is to make it easier to find programs that may apply to your situation, even if they are not disability-related. Each listing will include a short, plain-language description and a direct link to the official government source for accurate, up-to-date information.

Please check back soon as we continue to expand and organize this section.
